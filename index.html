CAT-MAN

Catch every mouse in the yard. Don't let the dogs catch you!

Please type a name to play.

How to play
  • Swipe up, down, left or right to steer the cat. On a computer, use the arrow keys or WASD.
  • Eat all the mice to finish the level. Each mouse is 10 points.
  • Eat catnip 🌿 and the dogs get scared. Catch them for 200, 400, 800 and 1600 points!
  • Grab the fish when it appears for bonus points.
  • You have 3 lives on every level. Each level the dogs get faster and smarter.
  • Press P or the pause button to take a break.
